# Changelog

## 1.0.6

- The popup after a kill or death no longer appears right away. It waits until you're out of combat and alive again, then shows a few seconds later.

## 1.0.5

- When several enemy players were targeting you when you died, the death is now saved as an "unknown player" right away. Before, it was lost if you closed the popup without picking anyone. Pick a name in the popup to move the death to that player, or click "Not a player" to remove it.
- To name the killer of an unknown-player death later, select it in the History tab and click "Record a death...".
- Deaths where a hostile mob was also on you are tagged "+mob". The History tab has a new "Mob" filter for them.

## 1.0.4

- No changes to the addon. This release adds this changelog to the CurseForge page.

## 1.0.3

- No changes to the addon.

## 1.0.2

- Left-click an alert to target the player (out of combat only). Right-click dismisses it.

## 1.0.1

- First release.
- WoW: Forever characters are stored by their full first name and surname.
